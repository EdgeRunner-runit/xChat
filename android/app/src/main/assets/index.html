How xChat works without internet.

What is xChat?

A private messenger that connects directly to people around you over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or local networks. It works even during network blackouts.

How do I add someone?

Share your username or address (shown on the Chats tab). Your contact enters it under "New chat" or scans your QR code.

Who can read my messages?

Only you and the person you message. No one in between can read them.

What are safety codes?

A 6-symbol code for each chat. Compare it with your contact to make sure no one is in the middle.

How do disappearing rooms work?

Messages are kept in temporary memory and are deleted completely when everyone leaves.

What does the emergency alert do?

Sends a call for help to xChat users nearby. Hold the alert button for 2 seconds to send.

What do the status labels mean?

Working means ready to send right now. Experimental means a feature being tested.

How xChat keeps you private

xChat is an offline-first private messenger designed for stressful situations and network blackouts.

Direct connection: Your messages travel straight from your phone to the other person over your local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. No central company or server sees or stores them.

Private by design: Everything you send is locked so only you and the other person can read it.

Safety codes: Each chat has a simple 6-symbol code. Compare it with your contact to be sure you are talking directly with no one in between.

Emergency alert: Sends a call for help to xChat users nearby when you need assistance.

Disappearing rooms: Messages in these rooms are deleted completely when everyone leaves.

Ways to send: If internet or Wi-Fi is down, xChat can send messages over Bluetooth, text messages (SMS), or sound.

Technical details
Version: 0.2.0 (Offline-First)
Core: Rust (xchat-core, xchat-crypto, xchat-transport)
Icons: Lucide (ISC License), Tabler (MIT License)
Security: Double Ratchet forward secrecy, ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption, X3DH key agreement
